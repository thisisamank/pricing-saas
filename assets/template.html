How to set a price

Do these three checks in this order.

  1. Value comes first. Calculate the money that the product saves or earns for the customer. Charge about one third of it, between 25% and 50%. The customer keeps the rest.
  2. Cost is only the floor. Do not start with cost, because that makes the price too low. Use cost to find the lowest price that you can accept. Keep a gross margin of 80% to 90%.
  3. Competition comes last. Do not start a price war. Make your product different, so that a customer cannot compare only the price.
